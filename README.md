# The Symphony of Silence

This comedic entry chronicles a seeker's ambitious quest for inner peace at a silent meditation retreat, only to be hilariously betrayed by the deafening acoustic reality of their own left knee. The humor lands instantly by sharply contrasting lofty spiritual transcendence with the unavoidable, mundane indignity of a clicking joint.
